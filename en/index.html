Welcome

A friendly base on the liveliest square in town

Historic Leiden outside the door, a comfortable bed behind it.

Hotel Mayflower sits right on the Beestenmarkt, the bustling square where Leiden meets for coffee, dinner and drinks by the water. Leiden Central Station is about five minutes away on foot, and the museums, canals and shopping streets of the old centre are all within easy walking distance.

We are a two-star city hotel in a historic Dutch building: honest comfort, a warm welcome and one of the best locations in Leiden, without the frills you would rather not pay for. Some of our rooms look out over the square, and some have a balcony, depending on the room type.

Discover Leiden

The view from your window

Leiden, City of Discoveries

Museums, canals and four centuries of stories.

Rembrandt was born here, the Pilgrims set sail from here, and the Netherlands' oldest university still sets the city's rhythm. Watch the market being set up on the square below, take a boat out on the canals, or wander the hofjes. Leiden rewards the curious.

At the reception we are happy to point you towards our favourite spots: the best coffee, the prettiest walking routes and the museums worth your morning.

Questions

Frequently asked

Does the hotel serve breakfast?

Not at the moment. Breakfast is temporarily unavailable while renovation works are ongoing. You will find several excellent cafés and breakfast spots within a short walk of the hotel, and we gladly point you to our favourites. If breakfast was included in your booking or paid in advance, the amount is of course refunded.

How far is the hotel from Leiden Central Station?

About five minutes on foot. Leave the station on the centre side, follow Stationsweg towards the city and you will walk straight onto the Beestenmarkt.

Can I arrive after 18:00?

Yes. If you let us know in advance, we place your key in our key locker so you can let yourself in at any hour. With your key you have 24/7 access to the hotel throughout your stay.
