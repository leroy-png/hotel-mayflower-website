Before you choose

Honest notes on our rooms

Our building dates from a time before elevators: all rooms are reached by stairs. If stairs are difficult for you, please contact us before booking so we can think along with you.

Views, balconies and exact layouts differ from room to room and depend on availability: some rooms overlook the Beestenmarkt, some have a balcony, others face the quieter rear. On warm days you will find a portable air cooler in your room; it is not air conditioning, but with the supplied ice packs it keeps things comfortable.

Rates are always live on our booking page; what you see there is what you pay, with no booking-site commission in between.
